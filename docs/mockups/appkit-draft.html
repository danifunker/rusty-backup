Rusty Backup on macOS: AppKit draft

The Inspect screen with an APM disk open, laid out like the Win32 shell (D8: same controls, same labels, same order), with macOS's own widgets. The second half proposes a menu bar that carries the same commands, since that is where Mac users look and Commander's F-keys need fn on a laptop. Nothing here is built yet beyond slice 0's window, toolbar and log.

New in this draft. Every item runs the same controller action as its button, and greys out when the button does. The shortcuts are proposals: they go into the controller's key map, not the shell.

Decisions for you

1. Keep the top bar, or move it to the menus?

  • Win32 has Refresh Devices, Settings... and About Rusty Backup as buttons; drawn here at the bottom right.
  • On a Mac, Settings and About live in the app menu. Option A keeps the buttons too (D8 parity); option B drops them from the window and relies on the menus.

2. Disk and Partition menus

  • They change with the screen: on Backup they would hold Backup's commands, and they hide on screens with no disk.
  • Alternative: one "Inspect" menu that is always there and greys out off the Inspect screen.

3. Shortcuts

  • Cmd 1 to 8 for the screens, Cmd O / Shift Cmd O to open, Cmd R re-inspect, Cmd E export, Cmd B browse.
  • Commander keeps its F-key map; its menu (slice 3) lists each with its F-key.

4. Look

  • Standard Mac push buttons, a source-list style table with zebra rows, a custom-drawn partition bar with the selected partition outlined, follows Light and Dark (use the button at the top).
